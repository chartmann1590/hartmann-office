# Hartmann Office privacy policy

Effective November 18, 2026.

Hartmann Office does not collect, send or sell any information.

- **No network.** The app has no network permission and makes no connections.
- **No account.** There is nothing to sign in to.
- **No tracking or analytics.** The app keeps no usage statistics and contains no third-party code that does.
- **Your files stay on your device.** Documents, spreadsheets and presentations are stored in the app's own
  folder inside its sandbox. A file leaves that folder only when you choose Save a copy, Export as PDF or
  Print, and it goes only where you choose in the system's file picker or print dialog.
- **Removing your data.** Files you delete wait 30 days in Deleted, then are removed. When you uninstall the
  app, Luma asks whether to keep or remove its data; removing it deletes every file the app stored.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/chartmann1590/hartmann-office/issues.
