# Hartmann Office privacy policy

Effective November 19, 2026.

Hartmann Office does not collect, sell or share information about you or your files.

- **Your files stay on your device.** Documents, spreadsheets and presentations are stored in the app's own
  folder inside its sandbox, with their earlier versions. A file leaves that folder only when you choose Save a
  copy, Export as PDF or Print, and it goes only where you choose in the system's file picker or print dialog.
- **No account.** There is nothing to sign in to.
- **No tracking or analytics.** The app keeps no usage statistics and contains no third-party code that does.
- **Update checks.** About twice a day at most, the app asks GitHub for its latest release so it can tell you
  about a new version and show the release notes; if you choose to download it, the file comes from GitHub too.
  GitHub sees your IP address and that the request came from Hartmann Office. Turn automatic checks off in the
  update panel (Check for updates in the app menu).
- **Office Pro.** The only other connections are for Office Pro, the optional purchase. When you open the Office Pro panel it fetches the prices. If you buy or restore it, the email you type
  goes to the Hartmann Studios license server and to Stripe, which runs the checkout in your browser and sends
  your receipt. The license server keeps your email and what you bought so you can restore it on another device;
  Stripe keeps your payment details under [its own privacy policy](https://stripe.com/privacy). The app never sees
  your card, and it never sends your files or anything in them. If you have Office Pro, the app checks your
  license again now and then so a cancelled subscription ends. If you never open Office Pro, it makes no Office Pro
  connections.
- **Removing your data.** Files you delete wait 30 days in Deleted, then are removed with their earlier versions.
  When you uninstall the app, Luma asks whether to keep or remove its data; removing it deletes every file the app
  stored, and your license on this device.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/chartmann1590/hartmann-office/issues.
